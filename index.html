01 / COLLECTION

Relics you can mint

Every Genesis Relic is a 1/1 retro 16-bit pixel art NFT — Owl, Fox, Deer, Raven, and 7,773 other generative variants. Rarity determines how much $TALV you receive. Minting draws your Relic at random from the remaining pool — a Legendary can land in any wallet, on any mint, and nobody can snipe a specific Relic.

The full collection — every Genesis Relic is generated from 9 pixel traits, and no two Relics are exactly alike.

02 / HOW IT WORKS

Three steps to the pool

01
FIRST 777 CLAIMS · FREE

Claim

Free, 1 Relic per Agent ID: 48,888,800 $TALV per claim. Paid minting runs in parallel from the very start — it never waits for the free claims to finish.

02
OPEN FROM #1 · 0.0015 BNB

Mint

0.0015 BNB per Relic, max 5 per wallet (+1 quota for every friend you invite who paid-mints). The 488,888,000 $TALV lands in your wallet in the same transaction as the mint.

#7,777 · AUTOMATIC

Pool Opens

The same transaction pairs 8.61 BNB (82% of the 10.5 BNB total genesis funds) with $TALV on PancakeSwap V2 and burns the LP. After that, the price ladder opens for everyone.
